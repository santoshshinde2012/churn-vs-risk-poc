# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be straightforward and be kind. Argue about the numbers, not about the person bringing them.

## The longer version

This project exists because several confident claims turned out to be wrong when they were
measured. That only works as a way of operating if people feel free to say "I ran this and I
got something different" without it being a confrontation.

**Expected:**

- Disagree with results, methods and conclusions freely, and bring evidence.
- Assume the other person has a reason for what they did, and ask what it was.
- Accept correction the way you would want it given: plainly, once, and then move on.
- Say when you do not know.

**Not accepted:**

- Personal attacks, harassment, or demeaning comments of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption, or dismissing someone's contribution on the basis of who they are
  rather than what they wrote.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere someone is representing the
project.

## Enforcement

Report anything that concerns you by opening a private security advisory (which is the only
private channel this repo has) or by contacting the maintainer directly. Reports will be
handled discreetly. Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions that do not follow
this document, and may ban repeat or serious offenders.
